📐 Formula & Metric Documentation

Completion Rate Denominator: The completion rate is calculated using NumPy vectorized conditions: Completion Rate = (Completed Enrolments / Total Enrolments) × 100. The denominator is strictly the total number of registered enrolments. If zero enrolments exist, it yields 0.0%.

14-Day Inactivity Threshold Definition: An active enrolment is flagged as inactive if the learner has recorded zero platform learning activities in the past 14 days (or since enrolment date if never active). Note: Missing activity is treated as an actionable pedagogical signal for instructor check-in and engagement support, not definitive proof that learning has stopped.
